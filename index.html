Sword Art Online Memory Defrag

A fan-made browser port of the game.

Unofficial project. Made by fans, non-profit, and not affiliated with Bandai Namco or the owners of Sword Art Online. This page contains no game files: assets come from your own copy of the APK and stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Ready to play

Your browser has not marked these files as persistent. If it runs out of space, it may delete them and you will need to import them again.

Play

Some APKs omit historical scout posters and introduction images. You can add the original resource pack without replacing your save.

1. Import your APK

Choose your saomd8.apks file (SAO MD Revival) or the game APK. Your browser reads the key from the game library, decrypts the assets, and generates the game data. This takes a few minutes and uses about 4 GB of disk space.

Use a desktop computer with Chrome, Brave, or Edge and about 4 GB of free space. Full Firefox and Safari support is still pending; touch controls are not available yet.
